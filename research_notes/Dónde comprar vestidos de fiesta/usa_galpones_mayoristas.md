# Galpones y mayoristas de ropa usada/excedente en EE.UU. (NY/NJ, Miami, LA, Houston) como fuente de vestidos de fiesta

Research notes, late Nov 2026. Method note: in this session, direct page fetches were blocked for most supplier domains (goodwillnynj.org, gciatl.com, raghouse.com, hissenglobal.com did not resolve). Most findings come from search-result summaries and have not been checked against the full pages. Many "guides" in this niche (closo.co, indetexx.com, hissenglobal.com) are SEO or vendor content. Their price figures are directional only and are flagged as such below.

## 1. Named warehouses/wholesalers in NY/NJ: who they are, where, public vs B2B, minimums

### Takeaway
NY/NJ is a major U.S. sorting and export hub for used clothing. The named graders/rag houses (e.g., Trans-Americas in Clifton, NJ) are B2B, sell by bale or truckload, and are built around mixed export grades, not formalwear. The only places in NY that are open to the public and charge by the pound are retail-style outlets: Goodwill's Long Island City "bins" and a few vintage pound shops. None of them is a reliable source of party dresses.

### Cited Findings
- **Trans-Americas Trading Co. (Tranclo)**, 50 Carol Street, Clifton, NJ. In business since 1942. Its group includes Kent Trans-Americas Clothing Corp and Trans-Americas Textile Recycling Co. It describes itself as a processor of secondhand clothing, vintage clothing, wiping rags and fiber-recycling materials. A Blackbook profile reported about 70,000 lb of clothing received per day. — [ScrapMonster listing](https://www.scrapmonster.com/company/trans-american-trading-co/55845); [Closo "Tranclo review"](https://closo.co/blogs/other/tranclo) (SEO blog); [NBC New York, "Vintage Mecca in — That's Right — New Jersey"](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/local/thread-vintage-mecca-innew-jersey/1881557/)
- I could not find Trans-Americas' actual grade list or category list (for example, whether it has an "evening/formal" category). The Closo review says each supplier applies its own internal grade definitions. — [Closo Tranclo review](https://closo.co/blogs/other/tranclo)
- An industry guide (vendor content) calls NY/NJ the largest U.S. export hub for used clothing, with sorting plants and container access to Africa and Europe. It names "American Used Clothing" in NJ as a key exporter. — [Hissen Global, used clothing export guide 2026](https://hissenglobal.com/used-clothing-export-complete-guide-global-trade-2026/); [Hissen Global, used clothing wholesale B2B guide 2026](https://hissenglobal.com/used-clothing-wholesale-complete-b2b-guide-importers-2026/)
- An Indetexx listing cites a **40,000 lb minimum order** for bales, a typical container-scale minimum for graders. — [Indetexx](https://www.indetexx.com/?p=28894)
- **Raghouse.com** sells used clothing in **1,000 lb increments** and has an in-person shopping page. I could not confirm its location or prices because the fetch was blocked. — [Raghouse in-person shopping](https://raghouse.com/pages/in-person-shopping); [Raghouse exclusive used clothing](https://raghouse.com/pages/exclusiveusedclothing)
- **Goodwill Outlet (bins), Long Island City, Queens**: 47-47 Van Dam St., LIC, NY 11101, tel. (718) 392-0125. Open to the public, priced by the pound. The only published prices I found are old (2011): clothing 1–24 lb = $1.69/lb, 25–49 lb = $1.49/lb, 50+ lb = $1.29/lb. A 2009 article confirmed the $1.69 first tier. — [QNS 2009](https://qns.com/2009/07/goodwill-opens-outlet-store-in-lic); [Sammy D Vintage 2011](https://sammydvintage.com/2011/12/nyc-goodwill-best-vintage-deals/)
- For comparison, current Goodwill outlet rates elsewhere: Greater Maryland $1.99/lb under 50 lb and $1.59/lb over 50 lb; another outlet network lists $1.89/lb for textiles (as of Dec 1, 2024). Apartment Therapy puts typical pay-by-the-pound pricing at $1.50–$2/lb. — [Goodwill Greater MC outlets](https://www.goodwillgreatermc.org/shopping/outlet-stores); [Apartment Therapy](https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/goodwill-clearance-center-bins-shopping-tips-37279828)
- Goodwill NYNJ's FAQ (per search summary) says professional resellers, vintage sellers and online sellers shop the same sales floors and Outlet bins as everyone else. There is no special wholesale channel for the public. It also runs an online auction storefront. — [Goodwill NYNJ FAQ](https://www.goodwillnynj.org/faq/); [ShopGoodwill NYNJ](https://shopgoodwill.com/NYNJ)
- **Dusty Rose Vintage (NYC)** opened as a vintage wholesale warehouse in 2012 and later opened to the public on weekends. It sources from used-clothing processing factories. The exact current location and status are unconfirmed. — [Time Out NY](https://www.timeout.com/newyork/shopping/dusty-rose-vintage)
- **FABSCRAP** (Brooklyn Army Terminal, Sunset Park) recycles textiles. It handles designer deadstock fabric, not clothing, and does not offer wholesale discounts. It is not a source of dresses. — [FABSCRAP](https://fabscrap.org/about); [Brooklyn Eagle](https://brooklyneagle.com/articles/2018/04/12/video-fabric-recycling-nonprofit-fabscrap-moves-to-brooklyn-army-terminal/)

### Inferences
- The realistic split in NY/NJ is: (a) B2B graders (Clifton/Passaic/Newark/Elizabeth corridor) with minimums from 1,000 lb up to full containers (about 40,000 lb), and (b) public pound outlets (Goodwill LIC) where you dig by hand. For a small Montevideo rental business, (a) is too large and too mixed unless a grader sells a specific "formal/evening" category, and (b) takes a lot of time for a low hit rate.
- Phone calls or visits to the Clifton/Passaic graders, asking specifically for "evening/formal/prom" sorts, are needed. That information is not public online.

### Gaps
- No public online list of NJ graders with addresses and minimums (Brooklyn/Bronx/Newark/Elizabeth/Passaic rag houses). I did not find specific named Brooklyn or Bronx rag houses.
- Current (2026) Goodwill LIC per-pound price. Goodwill NYNJ also operates other outlets (e.g., NJ), but I could not confirm locations because the site was blocked.
- SMART (Secondary Materials and Recycled Textiles association) member directory not consulted.

## 2. Formal/evening/prom categories: prices per lb/kg or per piece, bale sizes

### Takeaway
I found **no NY/NJ grader that publicly lists an "evening gown/prom/formal" bale with a price.** Published bale pricing covers generic grades. Formal dresses show up as (a) a specialty line at some exporters (e.g., "wedding dresses" at Global Clothing Industries), (b) per-piece lots on eBay or reseller marketplaces, or (c) new or sample stock from factories and boutiques.

### Cited Findings
- Generic U.S. bale price ranges, FOB (vendor guide): **Grade A/premium $2.50–$4.50/kg; Grade B $1.20–$2.00/kg; mixed unsorted $0.60–$1.20/kg.** Bales typically weigh 45–100 kg. A 20 ft container holds about 10–12 t (250–300 bales). — [Hissen Global, bales guide 2026](https://hissenglobal.com/used-clothes-bales-complete-guide-grades-pricing-sourcing-2026/) (vendor content, unverified)
- A reseller blog gives **$0.60–$1.80/lb** for bales, with "cream/vintage" grades at the top ($1.20–$1.80/lb) and mixed at $0.60–$1.00/lb. It warns that per-pound price matters less than the share of sellable pieces and the sorting labor. — [Closo, used apparel wholesale 2026](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/used-apparel-wholesale) (SEO blog)
- **Global Clothing Industries (gciatl.com, Atlanta area)** sells by the pound and ships monthly container loads to Latin America, Africa, the Middle East and Europe. Its listed specialties include **wedding dresses**. — [GCI products](https://gciatl.com/our-products); [GCI](https://gciatl.com/?p=2701) (site fetch blocked; from search summary)
- Per-piece examples: an eBay listing for a "Wholesale Lot of 1000 Prom Gowns" (new without tags); a used dress lot on Closo at $500 all-in, or **$10/unit**. — [eBay listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/174275342219); [Closo lot](https://closo.co/tools/closo/lot/1899997862)
- New wholesale prom gowns on Faire carry minimum orders, e.g., $629.17 minimum for one mermaid style and $90 minimum for a corset prom dress. — [Faire](https://www.faire.com/product/p_avhugxbpvf); [Faire](https://www.faire.com/product/p_tdfa3ttd7z)
- Brydeal of Factory runs a wholesale wedding-dress and formal-gown **sample sale** with discounts meant to cover alterations, repairs or cleaning. — [Brydeal sample sale formal dresses](https://brydealofactory.com/collections/sample-sale-formal-dresses)
- The Dress Outlet has a wholesale collection of formal dresses. — [The Dress Outlet wholesale](https://www.thedressoutlet.com/collections/wholesale)
- Grade definitions vary by sorter. U.S. sorters use about 50–80 categories versus 300–500 in European plants, so a fine category like "evening gowns" is less likely to exist as a standard U.S. sort. — [Indetexx grading](https://www.indetexx.com/?p=49743); [Indetexx](https://www.indetexx.com/?p=49462)

### Inferences
- For party dresses specifically, buying **per piece or per curated lot** (sample sales, deadstock prom lots, closing-boutique lots) is more predictable than per-pound bales. Formal dresses are heavy (beading, layers), which hurts per-pound economics compared with light tops.
- If a grader has a "formal/prom" sort, expect it to be priced well above generic cream ($/lb). Ask for photos or a video of the actual bale.

### Gaps
- No verified $/lb for a formal or prom-specific bale from any named supplier.
- No confirmed "cream/premium" category list from a NY/NJ grader.

## 3. Quality: grade A/cream vs mixed, and how much is usable designer/premium dress

### Takeaway
Even "good" bales yield only about 40–67% sellable items in reseller reports. Premium designer party dresses are a small share of a generic mix. Dresses also carry higher defect and return risk (stains, broken zippers, missing beads). Expect to have to do a trial purchase.

### Cited Findings
- A reseller reports bales ranging from about two-thirds sellable down to one that barely cleared 40%. — [Closo, used clothing wholesalers](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/used-clothing-wholesalers) (blog)
- Dresses return at a noticeably higher rate than basics, according to a reseller. A vendor blog reports that only 72% of incoming used prom dresses meet its standards. — [Closo, how to buy dresses wholesale](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/how-to-buy-dresses-wholesale); [Closo, used prom dresses economy](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-logistics-management/the-secret-economy-of-used-prom-dresses-how-to-save-or-make-hundreds) (vendor blogs)
- One "Grade A" can mean 90%+ new at one supplier and something else at another. Get written grade definitions. — [Indetexx](https://www.indetexx.com/?p=49376); [Apparel Search directory](https://www.apparelsearch.com/wholesale_clothing/wholesale_used_clothing.htm)
- Guides recommend ordering a trial bale first. — [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/used-clothing-wholesalers); [Indetexx, how to buy bales](https://www.indetexx.com/?p=17168)

### Inferences
- In a generic women's bale, party/formal dresses are probably a single-digit share, and designer ones fewer still. I found no source that measures this, so treat it as an estimate.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on the share of formal/designer dresses in cream bales.

## 4. Exports to Latin America and export logistics

### Takeaway
U.S. graders export heavily to Latin America. Miami and Houston (along with Laredo) are the main gateways, and several firms list LatAm as their core market. The bale/container model is designed for that trade. Uruguay's import rules for used clothing must be confirmed before buying: I found a textile import-licensing decree that mentions used clothing, but no confirmed outright ban.

### Cited Findings
- Miami is described as the main gateway for U.S. used-clothing exports to Latin America and the Caribbean. — [Hissen Global US market guide 2026](https://hissenglobal.com/us-used-clothing-wholesale-market-guide-chicago-miami-new-york-florida-2026/) (vendor; fetch blocked)
- **Miami:** TKClothes is described as strong in Grade A "tropical mixes" for LatAm and Africa (low-quality listicle). — [Indetexx, top Miami suppliers](https://www.indetexx.com/?p=45938). Mangy's Exports (es/LatAm-facing site) offers "cargas mixtas" and "cargas credenciales". — [Mangy's cargas mixtas](https://mangystexports.com/en-sv/pages/cargas-mixtas)
- **Houston:** Mangy's Textile Export has a Houston warehouse with pickup; most of its clients resell in Latin America. — [Mangy's about](https://mangystexports.com/en-hn/pages/acerca-de-nosotros). ZABS International exports used clothing to South and Central America. — [MapQuest ZABS](https://phoenix.aws.mapquest.com/us/texas/zabs-international-llc-410206423). Overseas Import Export Inc. is a family-owned Houston distributor of used clothing, shoes and rags. — [MapQuest](https://phoenix.aws.mapquest.com/us/texas/overseas-trading-inc-351044508). American Textile Export is near the Houston Ship Channel. — [MapQuest](https://phoenix-0af34b1.aws.mapquest.com/us/texas/american-textile-experts-417027218). Houston "pacas de ropa" buyer guide (blog, conflicting prices). — [Closo pacas Houston](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/pacas-de-ropa-en-houston)
- Guatemala imports about 250 million lb of used U.S. clothing a year. Megapaca is the largest buyer, a sign of the scale of the LatAm bale trade. — [Bloomberg 2023](https://brp-prod-bcc.bloomberg.com/news/features/2023-09-15/megapaca-wants-to-sell-used-clothing-back-to-americans)
- **Uruguay:** Decreto 432/2012 regulates imports of certain textile products. Its preamble mentions charity shipments of used clothing packed in bulk, and it sets up a prior-licence regime. Ley 17.887 (2005) banned imports of certain used goods, focused on vehicles. A 2026 MEF decree on postal shipments allows reasonable quantities of new or used goods for personal use. — [IMPO Decreto 432/2012](https://impo.com.uy/bases/decretos/432-2012/1); [IMPO Decreto 266/2013](https://www.impo.com.uy/bases/decretos/266-2013); [MEF 297/2026](https://medios.presidencia.gub.uy/legal/2026/decretos/03/mef_297.pdf); [Trade.gov Uruguay prohibited/restricted imports](https://www.trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/uruguay-prohibited-restricted-imports) (content not retrieved)

### Inferences
- Exporters can usually arrange container/LCL loads and FOB paperwork for LatAm buyers. Whether they will do a small LCL shipment of a few hundred kilos to Montevideo needs to be asked directly.
- **Regulatory risk is the critical blocker.** Before any commercial purchase of used clothing for Uruguay, confirm with a despachante de aduana whether used clothing (NCM 6309) can be imported commercially, and under which licence. If it is restricted, buying new or deadstock and sample stock (not "used") avoids the problem.

### Gaps
- Uruguay's current legal status for commercial imports of used clothing (NCM 6309) is unconfirmed. This needs an official source or a despachante.
- I found no named exporter that confirms shipments to Uruguay specifically, or small LCL minimums.

## 5. Real buyer experiences: is "incredible prices" realistic for quality party dresses?

### Takeaway
Prices per pound are low, but for party dresses the claim is misleading. The cost per *usable, rentable* gown is driven by the sell-through rate, the defect rate, sorting time, cleaning and alterations, and freight. I found no first-hand Reddit or YouTube reports of buying prom or evening-gown bales. Reseller content mostly warns about inconsistent grades.

### Cited Findings
- No Reddit threads specifically about prom/formal bales came up. Reddit prom finds are single thrift-store finds (e.g., a prom dress at Goodwill). — [The Cool Down](https://www.thecooldown.com/green-home/prom-dress-affordable-goodwill)
- Grade terms are not standardized and new buyers get burned. The first bale is where you find out whether a supplier's grading holds up. — [Closo used apparel wholesale](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/used-apparel-wholesale); [Closo bulk vintage 2026](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/bulk-vintage-clothing)
- Bale sell-through ranges from about 40% to 67% (see section 3). — [Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/inventory-liquidation/used-clothing-wholesalers)

### Inferences
- A rough illustration, not sourced: even at $10/unit for a used dress lot ([Closo lot](https://closo.co/tools/closo/lot/1899997862)), with about 50% usable you are at $20+ per usable dress before freight, duties, cleaning and alterations. That is still cheap compared with buying new, but it is not "incredible."

### Gaps
- No verified YouTube or Reddit first-hand accounts specific to formal-dress bales. A manual search of r/Flipping and r/ThriftStoreHauls with "bale dresses" is recommended.

## 6. Other U.S. options: bins, sample sales, bridal, Century 21, LA vintage warehouses

### Takeaway
For high-quality party dresses, the most promising U.S. channels are **sample sales** (260 Sample Sale, designer, bridal and bridesmaid sales in NYC; Kleinfeld's semi-annual sale) and **pound sales at curated vintage warehouses in LA** (about $6/lb). Off-price retail (Century 21) sells new goods at retail-level prices. All of these are buy-in-person and per-piece, with no export service.

### Cited Findings
- **260 Sample Sale (NYC):** usually 70–90% off, with a mix of public and private sales and a permanent store concept (Store260). A June 2026 article reports rising foot traffic as luxury shoppers look for value. Reviews mention bridesmaid dresses around $100. It expanded to a DC sale in mid-November 2026. — [260 Sample Sale about](https://260samplesale.com/pages/about-us); [Modern Retail, Jun 17 2026](https://www.modernretail.co/marketing/260-sample-sale-sees-foot-traffic-jump-as-luxury-customers-seek-value/); [Retail Brew](https://www.retailbrew.com/stories/inside-260-sample-sales-new-store-concept); [Atly reviews](https://www.atly.com/united-states/new-york/best-sample-sales); [PoPville Oct 2026](https://www.popville.com/2026/10/nyc-260-sample-sale-plans-washington-dc-sale-mid-november/)
- **Kleinfeld (Manhattan):** regular gowns cost $2,000 to $30,000+. A Time Out listing (undated) describes its warehouse sample sale at up to 85% off, sizes 6–28, from $499. A 2018 report has $4,000+ gowns at $999. Kleinfeld Again (its resale marketplace) joined the semi-annual sample sale (March 2025). Sales are by appointment (bride + 1 guest). I found no 2026 dates. Bridal gowns are only partly relevant to "vestidos de fiesta." — [Kleinfeld FAQ](https://KleinfeldBridal.com/help-faqs/); [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/newyork/shopping/kleinfeld-warehouse-sample-sale); [WeddingWire forum](https://www.weddingwire.com/wedding-forums/kleinfeld-bridal-sample-sale/91c91cd5035aa834.html); [Kleinfeld sample sale page](https://kleinfeldbridal.com/pages/bridal-gown-sample-sale)
- **Century 21 NYC** reopened in 2023 as a downsized flagship in the Financial District. It is off-price new merchandise, not wholesale. — [ABC7 NY](https://abc7ny.com/century-21-nyc-flagship-reopening/13253939/); [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/century-21-attracts-pre-pandemic-level-traffic-on-reopening-day)
- **American Rag Cie (LA):** a wholesale vintage warehouse in Commerce, CA, holding 3–4 million pieces in 20,000 sq ft. It is open by appointment with no buy-in or resale permit, and holds monthly "By the Pound" sales (June 2026 listing: $6/lb). I found no confirmation that formal dresses are part of the pound sale. **LA Vintage** warehouse sale at $6/lb (June 13, 2026, 13003 S Figueroa St). — [American Rag vintage wholesale](https://americanrag.com/vintage); [UncoverLA Jun 2026](https://uncoverla.com/2026/06/09/best-los-angeles-sample-sales-june-2026/); [Secret LA](https://secretlosangeles.com/massive-vintage-warehouse-sale-in-june/)
- Goodwill bins: see section 1 (LIC). Goodwill notes that designer and basic items cost the same per pound. — [Family Destinations Guide](https://familydestinationsguide.com/new-york-pound-thrift/)

### Inferences
- A practical plan for a Montevideo buyer: time a NYC trip around posted 260 Sample Sale and bridesmaid/evening designer sales, plus Kleinfeld's sample sale, and bring stock back as luggage or a consolidated courier shipment of new or sample goods. That probably beats bales on quality per dollar for rental-grade gowns.

### Gaps
- Lovely Bride, Chicmi listings and Salvation Army outlets were not researched in this pass.
- No 2026 dates or prices for Kleinfeld or for specific evening-wear brand sample sales.
